Questions

Good to know before you start.

Whose account does it trade on?

Your own. You create an API key in your Kalshi account and connect it in the bot. Your funds never leave your account — the bot only places orders through your key, and it can't withdraw or move money. Remove the key any time with /disconnect.

How much control do I have over how it trades?

Full control. You choose the coins, the strategy preset, how many contracts per signal, the price band you're willing to enter at, your exit rule, and your risk caps — daily loss limit, maximum open exposure, a balance floor it won't spend below, and how many trades it can take per window. You can stop it at any moment.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

No. The bot runs on our servers around the clock. There's nothing to install and nothing to babysit — you configure it once from Telegram on your phone and it keeps working while you sleep.

Do I need to trade real money?

Not to start. Paper mode runs the exact same logic against real live prices and simulates every fill, so you can watch it trade for as long as you like before connecting a Kalshi key. New accounts start in paper mode by default.

Is this guaranteed to make money?

No, and anyone who tells you otherwise is lying. Trading involves a real risk of loss, losing windows happen, and past or backtested results do not guarantee future results. The bot is a tool for executing a strategy consistently — it is not a promise of profit. Only trade money you can afford to lose.

If your strategy works, why sell access instead of just trading it yourself?

Both. Position size on 15-minute markets is capped by the liquidity in each window — past a certain size you move the price against yourself and the edge shrinks. Access fees are revenue that doesn't compete with our own fills, and they fund the infrastructure and ongoing research.

How do I pay and get access?

Pick a plan above and it opens the bot on that tier. Pay in BTC, ETH, USDT, USDC, SOL or another supported coin, and your key is issued automatically the moment the payment confirms on-chain. Redeem it in the bot with /redeem and you're trading.

Which coins and markets?

Kalshi's 15-minute crypto up/down markets: BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, DOGE, BNB, HYPE and more as Kalshi lists them. You pick which ones the bot watches for you.

Can I cancel or change plans?

There's no subscription to cancel — you buy a key for a fixed period and it simply expires. Buy another whenever you want, at any tier. Time from a new key stacks on top of whatever you have left.
