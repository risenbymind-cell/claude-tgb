This is not a performance claim. The recorded sample is a handful of 15-minute windows — far below the ~200 needed for any result to mean something. A high win rate here is expected and is not profit: Kalshi charges on entry and exit, so small targets can win nine times in ten and still lose money. Read the net column, not the win rate.

Fee model: ceil_to_cent(0.07 × contracts × P × (1−P)) per side, matching the exchange. Entries fill at the recorded ask, capped by resting size. Exits fill only when a later snapshot actually shows a bid at the target. Unfilled positions settle at the recorded outcome.
